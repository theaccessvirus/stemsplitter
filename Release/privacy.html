StemSplitter Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

On-device separation and saved music

StemSplitter separates selected media into vocals, drums, bass, and other instruments on your device. Local file splitting does not upload your media for separation. Completed stems and song metadata stay in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app. Device backups may include this storage according to your Apple backup settings.

Optional network features

Importing a Spotify or YouTube link contacts those services to find and fetch media. Those requests expose normal connection information, such as your IP address and the requested track or search, to the services involved. Their own privacy policies apply. Local media separation does not require those requests. Apple's media picker may also download your chosen media from iCloud.

Optional onlyStems Live

Live mode connects to your configured separation server. A paired capture device sends audio to that server, and the app receives separated stems over an encrypted connection. The preview service buffers audio in memory and does not save recordings. The service and network proxy may log connection metadata. Your access key is stored in this device's Keychain. Disconnect stops playback; the capture device has its own service controls.

Sharing and diagnostics

Exports go to the destination you select in the system share sheet. The app does not include advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or an app account system. During the beta, Apple TestFlight may provide crash reports and feedback to the developer under Apple's TestFlight privacy terms.

Device information used locally

The app checks free disk space before writing stems, reads timestamps of its own saved files to display history, and uses host time to synchronize playback. These values are not sent to the developer by the app.

Contact

Beta testers can contact the developer through TestFlight feedback. General questions can be submitted on the project issue tracker. Issues are public; do not include private recordings or personal information.
